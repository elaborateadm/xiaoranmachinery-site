Drilling Rig Selector

Enter your target depth, application and hole diameter — we match the right water well or mineral exploration rig from our catalog in seconds. No email required.

How the match works: the tool filters models by application and required hole diameter, then ranks rigs whose catalog depth covers your target with a 20–30% headroom margin — the safety buffer professional drillers allow for harder-than-expected geology. Catalog ratings are reference values, not a guarantee of drilling performance. Confirm geology, bit/rod size, compressor and configuration with our engineers before ordering.

Selection rules this tool applies

  • Depth headroom: catalog depth should be at least 1.2× your target depth. The best-sized match keeps the ratio between 1.2× and 1.8× — bigger is not always better, since oversized rigs cost more to buy, ship and run.
  • Hole diameter: if you need ≥ 250 mm holes, models capped at 219–254 mm are excluded automatically — matching the casing and screen program matters more than raw depth.
  • Mounting: crawler models handle rough, hilly or soft terrain; wheeled models win on road-rich sites and faster repositioning.
  • Exploration vs water well: core drilling for mineral exploration needs continuous coring capacity (FDX/FD series), while water wells need air/mud circulation and casing capability (FY/JDL series). They are not interchangeable.
