T480 Selection & Buying Questions

Use these answers with the published specifications to check the site, configuration and delivery requirements. Confirm the final figures against the agreed configuration, drawings and supplied manual.

Based on this page’s specification table · Updated October 4, 2026

What is the difference between T480 rated load and maximum load?

The brochure lists 350 kg rated load and 450 kg maximum load as separate fields. Do not use the maximum figure as a routine working rating. Confirm the permitted load, load centre and operating restrictions for the machine with the selected attachment.

What entrance width does the T480 require?

Published overall dimensions are 2169 × 1120 × 1300 mm, in length × width × height order. Verify the widest point with the actual bucket or attachment and allow clearance for turns, edges and uneven surfaces. A passage equal to the nominal width is not a verified access route.

Which attachments are compatible?

Choose the attachment for the task, then verify the mounting interface, attachment mass and allowed load. Powered tools also require confirmed auxiliary flow, pressure, hydraulic couplers, return or case-drain requirements and controls. This model table does not publish the complete auxiliary hydraulic specification; request it in the quotation rather than assume universal fitment.

What should be checked for delivery and unloading?

The brochure lists 1,321 kg operating weight for the T480. This is not a packed shipment weight. Confirm whether the bucket, other attachments, packaging and spares are included in quoted dimensions and mass, and match the transport vehicle and unloading equipment to that package.

How should I compare the tracked loader models?

Compare rated load, selected attachment, access width, operating weight and hydraulic compatibility. Model numbers alone do not rank capacity. Our C1200, V1000 and T480 comparison provides the current brochure figures as a starting point for a configuration-specific inquiry.
