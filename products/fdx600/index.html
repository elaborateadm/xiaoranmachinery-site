Choosing the FDX600 for core exploration

The FDX600 is listed for 600 m NTW drilling depth with 92 kW rated power and a 45–90° drilling angle. Compare the required core size and formation with the BTW and HTW depths in the table. Confirm the rod string, mud pump, winch and site access before ordering; published depths are configuration references, not a guarantee for every formation.
