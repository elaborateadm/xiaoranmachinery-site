Frequently asked questions

Why do NTW, BTW and HTW depth ratings differ for the same rig?

Depth depends on rod string diameter: thinner rods leave more room on the winch drum and reduce hoist load, so NTW depth is shallower than BTW at the same machine. See the full method in our NTW/BTW/HTW guide.

What should I send to get a rig recommendation?

Target depth, required core size (N/B/H), main formation types, terrain and access, power availability and destination country. With these we return a written configuration proposal.

How is the rig transported to my site?

Rigs ship folded inside a container with transport dimensions around 5.4 × 1.8 × 2.3 m for compact models. Check the transport table on this page against your route, and see our transport and site access checklist for the measurement method.

What circulation and winch configuration is typical?

A BW90-class mud pump and wireline winch sized to the target depth are the standard starting point; hard-rock or deeper programs may justify larger pumps and clamps — the configuration is confirmed in writing before production.
