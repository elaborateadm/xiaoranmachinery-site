GTJZ10 Selection & Buying Questions

Use these answers with the published specifications to check the site, configuration and delivery requirements. Confirm the final figures against the agreed configuration, drawings and supplied manual.

Based on this page’s specification table · Updated October 4, 2026

What are the GTJZ10 working height and platform height?

The published maximum working height is 12.0 m, and maximum platform height is 10.0 m. Platform height describes the platform floor; working height includes an allowance for the person working above it. If the task requires the platform floor at a specific elevation, select by platform height and verify the actual work position.

How much weight can the platform carry?

The listed platform rating is 320 kg. Count occupants, tools and materials together. The extension deck has a separate 113 kg limit; this is not an extra allowance added to the platform rating. Follow the supplied manual for occupant numbers, load distribution and extension use.

Which dimensions should be checked before delivery?

Listed overall size is 2.48 × 1.15 × 2.41 m; folded transport size is 2.48 × 1.15 × 1.97 m. Dimensions are length × width × height. Confirm the folded configuration, access clearances, floor loading and transport weight with the selected equipment.

Is gradeability the same as an allowed working slope?

The GTJZ10 lists 25% gradeability. This is not permission to raise or drive the platform on that slope. Confirm travel, levelling, wind and raised-platform restrictions in the supplied manual; the battery-electric chassis does not by itself establish outdoor suitability.

Can a spider crane replace this aerial work platform?

Do not assume interchangeability because a crane has an optional basket. Material handling and personnel access have different selection requirements. Verify the complete configuration, manufacturer-approved use and local requirements. State whether the task is moving material, positioning workers for installation or maintenance, or both.

Frequently asked questions

Why do NTW, BTW and HTW depth ratings differ for the same rig?

Depth depends on rod string diameter: thinner rods leave more room on the winch drum and reduce hoist load, so NTW depth is shallower than BTW at the same machine. See the full method in our NTW/BTW/HTW guide.

What should I send to get a rig recommendation?

Target depth, required core size (N/B/H), main formation types, terrain and access, power availability and destination country. With these we return a written configuration proposal.

How is the rig transported to my site?

Rigs ship folded inside a container with transport dimensions around 5.4 × 1.8 × 2.3 m for compact models. Check the transport table on this page against your route, and see our transport and site access checklist for the measurement method.

What circulation and winch configuration is typical?

A BW90-class mud pump and wireline winch sized to the target depth are the standard starting point; hard-rock or deeper programs may justify larger pumps and clamps — the configuration is confirmed in writing before production.
