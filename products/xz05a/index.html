XZ05A Selection & Buying Questions

Use these answers with the published specifications to check the site, configuration and delivery requirements. Confirm the final figures against the agreed configuration, drawings and supplied manual.

Based on this page’s specification table · Updated October 4, 2026

Can the XZ05A lift its rated load at maximum reach?

No capacity at maximum reach can be inferred from the headline rating. The XZ05A lists 5,000 kg rated load and 15.8 m fully extended boom length; these are separate specifications. Check the configuration-specific load chart at the actual radius, boom position and outrigger setup. Account for lifting gear and attachments as directed by the manufacturer.

What access and transport space does the XZ05A need?

The published product dimensions are 5,000 × 1,500 × 2,100 mm, in length × width × height order, and listed machine weight is 7,200 kg. Measure the doorway, overhead clearance, turning route and unloading area. These dimensions do not describe the deployed operating footprint. Confirm shipping dimensions and mass for the ordered configuration.

How much space is needed for the outriggers?

The XZ05A lists 6,600 × 6,100 mm maximum outrigger span and 4,900 × 5,100 mm minimum span. These figures screen site space; they do not establish equal lifting capacity in every position. Verify ground support and use the load chart for the actual deployment.

Are the remote control and work basket included?

Wireless remote control is listed as standard. A rotating work basket, jibs and glass suction attachment are optional and require separate confirmation. An optional basket does not establish approval for lifting people: confirm the complete manufacturer-approved configuration, operating limits and destination requirements.

What should I send for a model-specific quotation?

Send load weight and dimensions, lifting-gear weight, pickup and placement radii, required hook height, access width and height, available outrigger space, and site photos. Include the destination country or port so the machine, accessories and shipping arrangement can be specified together.

Frequently asked questions

Why do NTW, BTW and HTW depth ratings differ for the same rig?

Depth depends on rod string diameter: thinner rods leave more room on the winch drum and reduce hoist load, so NTW depth is shallower than BTW at the same machine. See the full method in our NTW/BTW/HTW guide.

What should I send to get a rig recommendation?

Target depth, required core size (N/B/H), main formation types, terrain and access, power availability and destination country. With these we return a written configuration proposal.

How is the rig transported to my site?

Rigs ship folded inside a container with transport dimensions around 5.4 × 1.8 × 2.3 m for compact models. Check the transport table on this page against your route, and see our transport and site access checklist for the measurement method.

What circulation and winch configuration is typical?

A BW90-class mud pump and wireline winch sized to the target depth are the standard starting point; hard-rock or deeper programs may justify larger pumps and clamps — the configuration is confirmed in writing before production.
