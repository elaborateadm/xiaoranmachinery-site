FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Buyer FAQ

Straight answers about ordering, payment, shipping, warranty and support for international buyers of XIAORAN MACHINERY equipment.

What is the minimum order quantity?

One unit. Mixed orders (different models, or machines plus spare parts and consumables) are accepted, and the packing plan is adjusted accordingly.

How do I get a quotation?

Send the machine model or application, target capacity (depth, load or working height), site conditions and destination country via the contact page or WhatsApp. You receive a written configuration and quotation — specifications are confirmed in writing before production.

What are the payment terms?

T/T is the most common arrangement; L/C can be discussed for established markets. Exact terms, deposit ratio and balance timing are stated in each quotation.

How long is the production lead time?

Typically 2–4 weeks after configuration confirmation, depending on model and optional equipment. The confirmed date is written into the order — see our three-step inquiry-to-delivery process.

How are the machines shipped?

Containerized sea freight is standard (see the packing walkthrough); oversized units ship by flat-rack or RO-RO. We quote FOB or CIF with your destination port.

What warranty applies?

Machines ship with a standard warranty covering manufacturing defects; wear parts are excluded. The exact scope and period for your machine are stated in the quotation and sales contract.

Are spare parts available after delivery?

Yes. A recommended wear-parts list ships with the machine, and parts supply continues after delivery — send the model and part description to the support contact.

Do you provide CE or other certificates?

Our certificate portfolio covers product compliance, management systems and CE documentation. Send your target market and machine model and we will prepare the document set for review — see certifications.

Can machines be customized for my market?

Yes. Power systems, electric standards, remote-control options and selected compliance configurations can be discussed according to destination and working conditions, and confirmed in writing before production.

Can I visit the factory before ordering?

Welcome. International customers inspect equipment and discuss configurations directly with our technical and export team in Jining, Shandong — see customer visits and about us.

Do you work with local distributors?

Regional partners are welcome. Send your market, product focus and company background to start the discussion.

What information makes an inquiry faster to answer?

Model (if known), target capacity, terrain or site constraints, power preference and destination country. With these five items we can usually return a configuration proposal in one business day.
