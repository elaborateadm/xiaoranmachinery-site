APPLICATION SCENARIO · COMPACT JOBSITE

Matching machines to a compact jobsite: skid steer, spider crane and scissor lift

Urban and indoor jobsites are decided by three numbers: the narrowest access width, the heaviest load, and the highest working point. Here is how we narrow down a machine list from those three numbers.

Step 1 — Measure the route, not just the site

A machine that fits the site but not the doorway never works. Measure gate width, corridor turns, floor transition strips and elevator dimensions. Our narrow access checklist and doorway clearance guide give the measurement method.

Step 2 — Match load to the real weight

Compare rated operating capacity against material density and attachment weight, not just bucket size — see rated capacity explained. For lifts, compare working height vs platform height.

Step 3 — Match lift to radius, not to headline tons

A spider crane's rated capacity applies only at a specific radius. The load chart guide and outrigger footprint checklist show how to read it for indoor floors.
