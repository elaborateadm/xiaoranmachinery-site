Mini Tracked Dumper vs Concrete Mixer: T500 and T550

Compare T500 tracked mini dumper and T550 tracked mini concrete mixer by job, maximum load, dimensions and site access.

Published September 29, 2026

The T500 and T550 share a compact tracked form, but they serve different jobs. The T500 carries and tips material in a cargo box. The T550 adds a mixing drum for on-site concrete work. Select by the task and the exact configured machine, not by the similarity of the model names.

What the brochure actually states

SpecificationT500 tracked mini dumperT550 tracked mini concrete mixer
Operating weight500 kg642 kg
Maximum load500 kg200 kg
Listed cargo box capacity0.22 m³0.145 m³
Overall dimensions (L × W × H)1735 × 844 × 1255 mm2155 × 800 × 1228 mm
Mixing drum diameterNot applicable600 mm

These are brochure figures. The listed T550 capacity is labelled “cargo box capacity” in the brochure; it should not be treated as a guaranteed concrete batch yield. Ask for the approved mixing volume and operating instructions for the selected configuration.

Choose the T500 for material transport

For moving soil, aggregate, debris or other suitable site materials along a narrow route, the T500 product page is a starting point. The brochure gives an 844 mm overall width and 500 kg maximum load. Check the material density, actual load, route condition and tipping area. The maximum figure is not a substitute for a verified operating procedure or any specific attachment rating.

Choose the T550 for mixing near the work

The T550 product page shows a compact tracked concrete mixer with a 600 mm drum diameter. Its 800 mm overall width may help with access screening, but the machine is longer than the T500. Ask how ingredients are loaded, what the approved batch quantity is and where mixed material will be discharged. Confirm cleaning access and water supply at the site.

Check the complete jobsite route

Measure gates and overhead clearances, assess ramps and bearing surfaces, and plan a safe place for turning and unloading. Tracked travel still requires a suitable ground condition. If the site needs both bulk material movement and concrete mixing, one machine description should not be stretched to promise both tasks; compare the two specific configurations and the workflow.

What to include in an inquiry

Send the material and daily volume, narrowest passage width, slope or step information, distance from unloading point to work area, desired engine configuration and destination country. Ask for the current machine drawing, operating limits and shipping dimensions for the exact ordered unit. For broader site equipment, browse the compact equipment range.
