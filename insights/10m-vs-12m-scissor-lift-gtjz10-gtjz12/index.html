10 m vs 12 m Platform Height: GTJZ10 and GTJZ12 Compared

Compare GTJZ10 and GTJZ12 working height, platform height, machine size, load and transport dimensions before choosing an electric scissor lift.

Updated October 20, 2026

Quick answer: GTJZ10 provides a 10.0 m maximum platform height and 12.0 m maximum working height. GTJZ12 provides 11.8 m platform height and 13.8 m working height. Select by the platform-floor elevation and actual work position, not by a model name alone.

GTJZ10 and GTJZ12 specifications

Choose platform height for the real work position

If the platform floor itself must reach 10 m, GTJZ10 matches that published figure. If the task requires more platform elevation, GTJZ12 lists 11.8 m. Working height includes an allowance above the platform and should not be treated as the floor height.

Compare access width and transport height

GTJZ10 lists a 1.15 m width and 1.97 m folded transport height. GTJZ12 lists a 1.19 m width and 2.10 m folded transport height. Measure doors, elevators, turning routes and loading ramps with clearance rather than comparing only the nominal widths.

Platform load is not the only operating limit

Both pages list a 320 kg safe working load and 113 kg extension-deck load. Count people, tools and materials together, and follow the supplied instructions for load distribution and extension use. Confirm indoor or outdoor suitability, floor capacity, slope, wind and charging conditions for the jobsite.

What to include in the inquiry

Send required platform-floor height, horizontal obstacles, number of occupants, tools and materials, access dimensions, floor or ground conditions, power availability and destination. Ask for the final configuration, transport dimensions, weight, battery and charger details, manuals and available documentation.
