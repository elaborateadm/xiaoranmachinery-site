How to Choose a 300 m Water Well Drilling Rig

Compare drilling method, hole diameter, air demand, feed stroke, geology and transport requirements before selecting a 300 m water well drilling rig.

Updated October 20, 2026

Quick answer: A 300 m depth label is only the starting point. Match the rig to the formation, hole diameter, drilling method, compressor output, rod length, access route and the real depth margin required for the project.

Start with formation and drilling method

Rock, unconsolidated ground and mixed formations do not create the same drilling requirement. State whether the planned method is DTH air drilling, mud rotary drilling, casing advancement or a combination. A model that reaches the target depth in one formation may need a different tool string, compressor or circulation plan in another.

The FY300A / FY300B page lists a 300 m catalog depth and a 140–325 mm hole-diameter range. These are catalog figures, not a guarantee for every geology and diameter. Ask for a configuration based on the bore profile.

Check the compressor as part of the system

For air drilling, compare the rig’s published working-air pressure and consumption with the compressor at the expected operating condition. The FY300A / FY300B table lists 1.7–3.0 MPa and 17–36 m³/min. Do not select a compressor from the maximum depth alone; hammer size, hole diameter, elevation, hose losses and formation affect the requirement.

Compare feed stroke and rod handling

FY300A lists a 3.3 m feed stroke, while FY300B lists 6.6 m. The longer stroke can reduce rod-handling cycles when the matching rod length and transport conditions are suitable. Confirm the drill-pipe diameter, length, handling method and loading height for the ordered configuration.

Allow depth margin without oversizing the whole project

If the planned hole is close to 300 m, compare the FY300 series with FY400 and JDL350 / XJDL350. The correct margin depends on geology, diameter and method; it is not simply the next larger model number. A heavier machine may also change transport, unloading and access requirements.

Send a complete inquiry

Provide target depth and final hole diameter, geology or nearby bore records, preferred drilling method, casing plan, rod specification, compressor already available, access width, site photos and destination. Ask the supplier to list the rig, tools, rods, compressor requirements, options, spares and shipping dimensions separately.

Request a configuration-specific recommendation

Send the required depth, load or height, jobsite conditions, access dimensions and destination. We will compare suitable models and options.
