FY300 vs FY400 vs FY500 Water Well Drilling Rig Comparison

Compare FY300A/FY300B, FY400 and FY500 catalog depth, hole diameter, engine power, air demand and transport factors for water-well projects.

Updated October 20, 2026

Quick answer: Choose the smallest configuration that provides adequate depth and diameter margin for the actual geology, while matching the available compressor, access route and transport plan.

FY300A and FY300B both list 300 m catalog depth, 140–325 mm hole diameter, 85 kW engine power, 1.7–3.0 MPa working pressure and 17–36 m³/min air consumption. Their feed strokes differ: 3.3 m for FY300A and 6.6 m for FY300B.

When FY300 belongs on the shortlist

Start with FY300A / FY300B for projects planned around the 300 m catalog class and compatible hole diameters. Compare the two feed-stroke options against rod length, handling frequency and transport arrangement.

When to compare FY400 and FY500

FY400 increases the catalog depth to 400 m and lists 92 kW power. FY500 lists 500 m, a 140–350 mm hole-diameter range and 118 kW power. Those larger figures do not remove the need to confirm the compressor and tool string; FY500 also lists a higher upper air-consumption figure.

Transport and site access still decide the result

Compare operating weight, loading width, overall dimensions, approach road and unloading equipment. A larger drilling margin can be useful, but oversizing can add freight and access constraints. Request packed dimensions and gross weight for the exact machine, rods, tools and options.

Use one comparison sheet for supplier quotations

Keep the same target depth, geology, diameter, rod program and compressor assumptions across every quotation. Ask suppliers to identify exclusions and optional equipment. This makes price differences easier to understand and reduces the risk of comparing incomplete configurations.
