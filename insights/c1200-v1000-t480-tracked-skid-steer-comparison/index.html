C1200 vs V1000 vs T480: Tracked Skid Steer Buyer Comparison

Compare C1200, V1000 and T480 rated load, operating weight, dimensions and bucket capacity, then confirm access and attachment compatibility.

Published October 4, 2026

Quick answer: Use the required rated load, actual attachment and access route to shortlist these tracked loaders. T480 has the lowest listed operating weight and length of the three; C1200 has the narrowest listed overall width; V1000 has the highest listed rated load. Their model names do not rank every specification.

It does not establish the payload with every fork, auger or other attachment. Confirm the final powertrain, accessories and dimensional drawing in the quotation.

Which loader has the narrowest listed width?

C1200 lists 1,045 mm overall width, compared with 1,120 mm for T480 and 1,240 mm for V1000. Those figures make C1200 worth reviewing where width is the first screening constraint. However, its 2,867 mm listed length is greater than T480’s 2,169 mm; a narrower machine may still need a different turning route.

Measure the complete route and the widest point of the selected attachment. A gate equal to the brochure width leaves no verified clearance. Include turns, overhead restrictions, surface edges and the unloading approach. See our narrow-access measurement guide for a route checklist.

Which model has the higher rated load?

V1000 lists 500 kg rated load, C1200 lists 440 kg and T480 lists 350 kg. Their separate maximum-load fields are 650 kg, 500 kg and 450 kg respectively. Use the rated-load field for the initial comparison; do not substitute the larger maximum figure for a routine working rating.

Define what will be handled: a loose material, pallet or powered attachment has different loading geometry. Ask for permitted loads with the actual attachment and load centre. Bucket volume is not itself a mass rating, so do not convert 0.19 m³, 0.15 m³ or 0.1 m³ into an allowed payload without configuration-specific confirmation.

Does a larger rated load guarantee attachment compatibility?

No. The model tables do not publish complete auxiliary hydraulic flow and pressure figures. A higher rated load therefore does not confirm that a powered tool will run correctly. Specify the mounting interface, attachment mass, flow range, pressure requirements, couplers, any case drain and control connections in the inquiry.

Browse the attachment catalogue to name the required tool, then request written compatibility confirmation. Bobcat’s attachment guidance illustrates the general principle that mounting, hydraulic and control requirements matter alongside flow; it is not evidence of cross-brand compatibility for XIAORAN models.

Compare the complete delivery package

T480 lists 1,321 kg operating weight, C1200 lists 1,825 kg and V1000 lists 1,892 kg. These are machine operating figures, not packed shipment totals. Ask whether the proposed bucket, optional tools, spares and packaging are included in transport mass and dimensions.

Send target rated load, material or attachment, passage measurements, route photos, site surface and destination. Request the machine and attachment specifications, loading arrangement and delivery term together. Link your inquiry to C1200, V1000 or T480 so quote revisions refer to the same configuration.
