Spider Crane Selection Guide: Capacity, Reach and Outrigger Space

Spider cranes combine a compact crawler chassis with folding outriggers and a telescopic boom. Their value comes from reaching restricted jobsites, but safe and productive selection requires more than choosing a rated tonnage.

Updated September 23, 2026

Use the load chart, not rated capacity alone

Rated load is normally available only at a short working radius and a specific boom position. Start with the actual load, lifting radius and required hook height, then check the corresponding load-chart value. Include the hook block, lifting accessories and an appropriate operating margin.

Measure the complete access route

Record doorway width, corridor width, turning space, floor loading, ramps and overhead restrictions from unloading point to lifting position. Compare these limits with transport width, folded height, machine weight and crawler dimensions.

Confirm outrigger deployment

A compact body still needs sufficient stabilizer space. Check maximum and minimum outrigger span and whether partial deployment is permitted by the selected model. Ground bearing capacity and leveling conditions must be evaluated before operation.

Choose controls and attachments for the task

Wireless remote control improves visibility and is standard on XIAORAN spider cranes. Depending on the project, optional proportional valves, load-moment limiter, manual or hydraulic jib, rotating basket and glass suction attachment can improve control and application range.

Compare total working configuration

Ask for boom length, maximum lifting height, rotation range, travel speed, engine or electric system, machine dimensions and weight. Final selection should be verified against the manufacturer load chart and the actual site plan.
