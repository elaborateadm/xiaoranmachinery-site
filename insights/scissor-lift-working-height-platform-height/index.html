Scissor Lift Working Height vs Platform Height: Choosing the Right Model

Understand the difference between working height and platform height, then compare load and chassis conditions.

Updated September 24, 2026

Separate the two heights

Working height generally includes an allowance for a person reaching above the platform. Platform height is the elevated deck level. Compare the specified height used by your site, rather than treating these figures as interchangeable.

Compare a real model

The GTJZ08 page lists 10.0 m maximum working height and 8.0 m maximum platform height, with a 450 kg safe working load. The GTJZ12 lists 13.8 m working height and 11.8 m platform height, with a 320 kg load.

Account for people and tools

The rated load must include occupants, tools and materials. Check extension-deck limits separately; they can differ from the main platform capacity.

Choose travel and ground setup

Measure access width and transport height, then confirm whether the floor is level and load bearing. A wheeled indoor lift and a crawler platform solve different site constraints; verify the exact chassis and power source before quoting.
