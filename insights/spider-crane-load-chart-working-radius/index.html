Spider Crane Load Chart: Rated Capacity vs Working Radius

Learn how working radius, boom extension and outrigger setup affect spider crane selection beyond its headline rated capacity.

Updated September 24, 2026

Rated capacity is a starting point

A spider crane’s headline tonnage is its maximum rated load under defined conditions. The permitted load changes with working radius, boom extension and setup. Select from the manufacturer’s chart for the exact configuration and never assume that maximum capacity applies at maximum reach.

Measure radius and the full load

Working radius is the horizontal distance from the crane’s rotation center to the load line. Provide the load weight plus any lifting gear, the pickup and placement radius, and the required hook height. These numbers help identify a suitable boom and lifting arrangement.

Check footprint and ground conditions

Measure access width and height for the crawler machine, then check the space needed to deploy outriggers. Verify supporting ground conditions and any site restrictions. The compact travel size does not represent the operating footprint.

Request the correct chart before purchase

Compare XZ03A, XZ05A or larger models against the actual lift plan. Ask for the chart matching the proposed boom, jib and outrigger configuration, and confirm the operating instructions and any destination requirements in the quotation.
