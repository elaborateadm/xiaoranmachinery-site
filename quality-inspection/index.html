Quality Inspection

Inspection content and test records depend on the model and order. Request the exact checklist and any available conformity documents before purchase.

01

Confirm the order

Match the model, options and stated technical parameters against the written quotation before work starts.

02

Check the assembled machine

For the agreed configuration, request images of assembly and a documented review of the visible components, controls and safety devices.

03

Witness operation

Ask for operating photos or video of the selected machine and agree which functions will be demonstrated before dispatch.

04

Confirm the handover

Record agreed inspection results, supplied accessories, manuals and the spare-parts list with the final packing scope.
